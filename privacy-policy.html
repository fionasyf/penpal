Penpal 隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026 年 7 月 14 日

欢迎使用 Penpal（以下简称"本 App"）。本 App 是一款帮助中文母语者提升外语表达的输入法键盘，支持英语、日语、韩语、粤语、法语、德语、西班牙语等多种语言。我们非常重视你的隐私。本政策说明我们如何处理你在使用本 App 过程中产生的信息。

一、我们不收集什么

本 App 不需要注册、不需要登录，我们不收集你的姓名、手机号、邮箱、位置、通讯录、设备标识符等任何用于识别你个人身份的信息，也没有用于用户追踪或广告的功能。

二、为提供功能而处理的内容

为了实现语法检查、翻译和语音识别等核心功能，本 App 会在你主动使用相关功能时，将以下内容发送至第三方 AI 服务进行实时处理：

  • 你输入的文本：当你使用语法检查或翻译功能时，你输入的文字会被发送用于分析并生成建议。
  • 你的语音录音：当你使用语音输入功能时，录制的音频会被发送用于转写成文字。

这些内容仅用于生成你当前请求的结果，处理完成后即返回给你使用。本 App 不会在你的设备上或我们的服务器上长期存储这些文本或录音，也不会将其用于识别你的个人身份。

三、第三方服务

为提供上述功能，本 App 使用了以下第三方 AI 服务。你的输入内容在你使用相应功能时会被传输至这些服务处理，其数据处理受各自的隐私政策约束：

  • DeepSeek（用于语法检查、翻译）——deepseek.com
  • Groq（用于语音识别）——groq.com
  • 硅基流动 SiliconFlow（语音识别的备用服务）——siliconflow.cn

我们建议你避免在使用本 App 时输入或朗读高度敏感的个人信息（如密码、银行卡号等）。

四、键盘的"完全访问权限"

本 App 的键盘扩展需要你授予"完全访问权限（Full Access）"，这是为了让键盘能够联网，从而调用上述 AI 服务完成语法检查、翻译和语音识别。若不开启该权限，键盘将无法连接网络，相关功能将不可用。我们仅将该网络访问用于本政策所述的功能，不会用它收集你的键入内容用于其他目的。

五、儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童设计，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

六、政策的变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。更新后的版本将发布在本页面，并更新顶部的"最后更新日期"。

七、联系我们

如你对本隐私政策有任何疑问，可通过以下方式联系我们：
邮箱：feiandpensieve@gmail.com

Penpal Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Welcome to Penpal (the "App"), a keyboard that helps Chinese speakers write more natural text in the language they are learning, including English, Japanese, Korean, Cantonese, French, German, and Spanish. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

1. What we do NOT collect

The App requires no sign-up and no login. We do not collect your name, phone number, email, location, contacts, device identifiers, or any information that identifies you personally. The App contains no tracking or advertising features.

2. Content processed to provide features

To provide grammar checking, translation, and speech recognition, the App sends the following to third-party AI services for real-time processing, only when you actively use the relevant feature:

  • Text you enter: when you use grammar check or translation, your text is sent for analysis and suggestions.
  • Your voice recordings: when you use voice input, the recorded audio is sent to be transcribed into text.

This content is used solely to generate the result you requested and is returned to you. The App does not persistently store this text or audio on your device or on our servers, and does not use it to identify you.

3. Third-party services

The App relies on the following third-party AI services. Your input is transmitted to them when you use the corresponding feature, and their processing is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • DeepSeek (grammar check, translation) — deepseek.com
  • Groq (speech recognition) — groq.com
  • SiliconFlow (fallback speech recognition) — siliconflow.cn

We recommend that you avoid entering or dictating highly sensitive personal information (such as passwords or bank card numbers) while using the App.

4. Keyboard "Full Access"

The App's keyboard extension asks you to grant "Full Access." This is required so the keyboard can connect to the internet and call the AI services above for grammar checking, translation, and speech recognition. Without it, the keyboard cannot access the network and these features will not work. We use this network access only for the features described in this policy and do not use it to collect your keystrokes for any other purpose.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
